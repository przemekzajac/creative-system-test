# Seedance 2.0 Mini prompt — 15 s cut (free-trial generation)

Used because the Artlist free trial does not cover Seedance 2.5. Same scenario
as `seedance-prompt.md`, compressed from 18 s to 15 s:
0–3 s problem · 3–6 s action · 6–11 s payoff · 11–15 s outcome.

Settings: Seedance 2.0 Mini, image-to-video (references), 9:16, 15 s, 720p,
audio on. Reference: `references/soap-hero.png` as `@Image 1`.

```text
[References]
@Image 1 defines the hero product scene: a cream-white rectangular organic soap bar standing upright on a rough oval travertine stone, a speckled beige ceramic vase with dried white baby's-breath on the left, a green olive branch at the top right, a cream linen cloth lower left, warm sand wall, soft morning light with leaf shadows. Keep its lighting, colours and composition. Hide the "safa" wordmark until the last stage.

[Generation Goal]
A 15-second vertical 9:16 social video ad for Canva's "Magic Eraser" feature. Photoreal, warm, airy, premium and friendly. Story: a perfect product photo is spoiled by a stray finger, it is removed in Canva with Magic Eraser, and the photo becomes post-ready. All on-screen text: English, spelled exactly as written, large bold white clean geometric sans-serif with a soft shadow, centred in the upper-middle of the frame, never in the top 14 % or bottom 35 %. Maximum two lines. No voiceover, no faces.

[0–3 s — The problem]
The exact scene from @Image 1 fills the frame; slow push-in. At 1.5 s a blurred human fingertip intrudes from the bottom-right corner, clearly accidental. Quick zoom-punch and a thin red hand-drawn circle around the finger.
Text: "This photo was perfect… until I noticed THAT."

[3–6 s — The action]
Cut to the same photo on the canvas of the Canva editor on a laptop screen, straight-on. Clean white interface, light-grey canvas, left white "Edit image" panel, the "Magic Eraser" tool highlighted in Canva purple (#8B3DFF), "Brush" mode and a brush-size slider, a wide rounded purple "Erase" button. A cursor paints a translucent purple highlight over the finger, then clicks "Erase"; the highlight shimmers with small sparkles.
Text: "Reshoot it? Nope."

[6–11 s — The payoff]
The finger dissolves and the stone and linen fill in seamlessly, no smudge. Cut to the clean photo full-frame; a vertical white wipe line slides left to right: left side "Before" with the finger, right side "After" clean. Then a slow push-in on the clean photo.
Text: "Just erase it."

[11–15 s — The outcome]
The clean photo becomes a finished product post: the teal-green "safa" wordmark with the caption "ORGANIC SOAP BAR" fades in above the soap exactly as in @Image 1. At 13 s a rounded purple pill button with white text appears in the middle of the frame, with a small purple-to-teal gradient rounded "c" logo and the word "Canva".
Text 11–13 s: "Post-ready. No reshoot required."
Button 13–15 s: "Clean up your photo in Canva"

[Maintain Consistency]
Same soap shape and colour, stone, vase, olive branch, linen, light from the upper left and warm palette in every shot. Text always in the same font and upper-middle position. Canva purple and teal exact.

[Audio]
(Light, upbeat instrumental acoustic-pop with soft claps, builds gently, clean ending at 15 s.)
<Soft "uh-oh" blip at 1.8 s.> <UI click, gentle brush swish, second click on Erase.> <Magic shimmer, smooth whoosh as the finger vanishes, success chime on "After".> <Soft chime when the button appears.>
No dialogue, no voiceover, no lyrics.

[Avoid]
Misspelled or extra text, watermarks, faces, extra people, other logos, flicker, camera shake, changes to the soap, text in the bottom third, black bars.
```
