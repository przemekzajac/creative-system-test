# Kling 2.5 Turbo Pro prompt — 10 s cut

Shortest variant, under Kling's 2,500-character prompt limit. The soap photo is
the start frame. No generated audio; add music in post.

Settings: Kling 2.5 Turbo Pro, image-to-video, 9:16, 10 s, 1080p.
Start image: `references/soap-hero.png`.

Negative prompt: `misspelled text, garbled letters, extra text, watermark, faces, extra people, other logos, flicker, camera shake, deformed soap, black bars, blurry, low quality`

```text
Vertical 9:16 social video ad for Canva's "Magic Eraser" feature, 10 seconds. Photoreal, warm, airy product photography. Opens on this exact soap still life: cream soap bar upright on a travertine stone, speckled vase with dried flowers, olive branch, linen, warm sand wall, soft morning light. Keep the soap, props and lighting identical throughout.

0-2 s: slow push-in. A blurred human fingertip intrudes from the bottom-right corner, clearly accidental; a thin red hand-drawn circle pops around it. Bold white text, upper-middle of frame: "This photo was perfect... until I noticed THAT."

2-4.5 s: cut to the same photo inside the Canva editor on a laptop screen: clean white interface, a left panel with the "Magic Eraser" tool highlighted in purple and a rounded purple "Erase" button. A cursor paints a translucent purple highlight over the finger and clicks "Erase"; sparkles shimmer. Text: "Reshoot it? Nope."

4.5-7.5 s: the finger dissolves and the stone and linen fill in seamlessly. Cut to the clean photo full-frame; a white wipe line slides left to right revealing the clean image. Text: "Just erase it."

7.5-10 s: the clean photo becomes a finished product post with the teal "safa" wordmark above the soap. A rounded purple pill button appears mid-frame. Text: "Post-ready. No reshoot required." Button text: "Clean up your photo in Canva"

All text in English, spelled exactly, large bold white clean sans-serif with soft shadow, centred in the upper-middle, never in the bottom third.
```
